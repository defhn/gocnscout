# gocnscout Services and Pricing

All prices are in USD and are reference prices. Scope and final price are confirmed with the customer before any payment.

## Free

- Company database search and company profiles (no sign-up)
- Industry pages and city pages
- Discord community for simple sourcing questions

Contact persons, phone numbers and personal emails are never published. CSV export of the database is not offered to visitors.

## Paid human services

Request at https://gocnscout.com/pricing#human-review

- Supplier Identity Check: from $149 (one supplier, desk research on public records)
- Buyer Decision Review: from $249 (one supplier, deeper review and buyer advice)
- Buyer Decision Review - 3 Suppliers: from $499 (up to three suppliers, deeper review and comparison)
- Data License: from $6,000/year (by enquiry, non-sensitive supplier dataset, internal use)

Reviews are first-pass research on public information. They do not guarantee that a supplier is safe or suitable for payment.
