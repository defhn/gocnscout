## 第 001 条原始制作提示词（用户已确认）

```text
Create a 1080×1920 vertical video introducing GoCNScout to overseas buyers
who want to research Chinese companies and suppliers.

Visual reference:
Use the style of the supplied 030 reference video: dark navy background,
large white text, compact information cards, highlighted keywords,
simple animated entrances, a progress bar, and synchronized subtitles.

Brand:
Show “GoCNScout” near the top from the opening.
Use the real GoCNScout logo and real website screen recordings.
Use teal and white as the main accent colors.
Use amber only for the limitation notice.

Structure:

1. Opening question:
   Display “Need to check a China supplier?”
   The text appears with a quick, clean animation.

2. Free database:
   Show the actual GoCNScout database page.
   Demonstrate searching a company name and opening one available record.
   Add the label “FREE COMPANY DATABASE”.

3. Available information:
   Zoom into the real company profile.
   Highlight the company name, location, and product description,
   only where those fields are actually present.

4. Paid human review:
   Show the actual human-services page.
   Highlight “Supplier Identity Check”.
   Add three short labels:
   “Company identity”
   “Public records”
   “Risk signals”
   Clearly label this as a paid human service.

5. Limitation:
   Display “A starting point, not a safety guarantee.”
   Add “Records are not individually verified.”
   Do not imply that directory entries are verified factories.

6. Ending:
   Show GoCNScout logo and “gocnscout.com”.
   Display “Search company records for free”.

Voice:
Use Gemini text-to-speech to generate a clear, natural English voice.
Tone: calm, credible, helpful, and professional.
Avoid a dramatic sales voice.
Speak at a pace that allows the viewer to read the screen.

Subtitles:
Burn English subtitles into the video.
Synchronize them with the actual generated audio.
Highlight one or two important words in teal or amber.
Keep all subtitles within the mobile-safe area.

Production:
Use actual website recordings, text cards, and simple programmatic
animations. Do not use a video-generation model.
Do not fabricate company information, review results, customer stories,
verification badges, or safety guarantees.
Avoid showing personal contact details.
```

说明：第 001 条实际样片采用网站截图与缓慢缩放，未使用连续屏幕录制；本批次同样以真实页面截图合成，实际呈现以 MP4 为准。
